Why Millet Cookies Are Healthier Than Maida Biscuits

Healthy Snacking • MILASTY Blog

Cookies are one of the most popular tea-time snacks. However, most biscuits available in the market are made using maida (refined wheat flour). While they taste good, maida-based biscuits often lack nutritional value and contain refined carbohydrates.

Millet cookies, on the other hand, are made from traditional grains like ragi and bajra. These grains are naturally rich in fiber, minerals, and nutrients, making them a healthier alternative to regular biscuits.

1. Higher Nutritional Value

Millets are considered one of the most nutritious grains. They contain important nutrients such as calcium, iron, magnesium, and antioxidants.

For example, ragi is extremely rich in calcium, which supports bone health. Maida loses most of its nutrients during the refining process.

Because of this, millet-based cookies provide more natural nutrition compared to maida biscuits.

2. Rich in Dietary Fiber

Millets are naturally high in fiber. Fiber supports digestion, improves gut health, and keeps you feeling full for longer.

Maida biscuits typically contain very little fiber, which means they can cause quick hunger after eating.

Millet cookies offer a more balanced snack that supports digestion and provides longer-lasting energy.

3. Lower Glycemic Impact

Millets generally have a lower glycemic index than refined flour. This means they release energy slowly into the bloodstream instead of causing rapid spikes in blood sugar.

Foods made with maida tend to cause sudden increases in blood sugar levels, which can lead to energy crashes later.

Millet cookies help maintain steadier energy levels throughout the day.

4. Traditional Grains Making a Comeback

Millets have been part of traditional Indian diets for centuries. In recent years, they are gaining popularity again as people become more conscious about healthy eating.

By using grains like ragi and bajra, millet cookies combine traditional nutrition with modern snacking convenience.

Choosing a Healthier Tea-Time Snack

If you are trying to reduce refined foods in your diet, switching from maida biscuits to millet cookies is a simple and effective choice.

While cookies should always be enjoyed in moderation, choosing snacks made from wholesome grains can make a meaningful difference in your daily diet.
