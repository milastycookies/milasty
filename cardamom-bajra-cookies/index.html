Cardamom Bajra Cookies

Homemade • Small batch baked • Made with desi ghee

Ingredients

Bajra flour, jaggery, whole wheat flour (atta), desi cow ghee, rice bran oil, milk powder, cardamom, sunflower seeds, baking powder, baking soda, salt.

Allergen Information

Contains whole wheat (atta) (gluten), milk, and sunflower seeds. Manufactured in a home kitchen where other nuts and seeds may be handled.

Why you’ll love it

  • Made with bajra (pearl millet), a traditional grain commonly enjoyed in Indian households as part of everyday meals.
  • Naturally flavoured with cardamom (elaichi), offering a warm, familiar aroma without artificial flavours.
  • Sweetened with jaggery instead of refined sugar, keeping the sweetness mild and balanced.
  • Prepared using desi cow ghee along with rice bran oil, avoiding palm oil and heavily refined fats.
  • Contains whole wheat flour (atta), not refined wheat (maida), keeping the grain closer to its natural form.
  • Baked in small batches with no preservatives, no artificial colours, and no flavour enhancers.

A gentle, everyday cookie inspired by traditional flavours.

Who is this for?

  • Families and parents looking for a wholesome snack option made with traditional grains and familiar ingredients.
  • New and young mothers who prefer gentle, homemade-style foods prepared in small batches with simple ingredients.
  • Fitness-conscious and active individuals who choose whole grains over refined ingredients for everyday snacking.
  • Anyone who enjoys mindful eating and values transparency in how their food is made.

Enjoy as part of your everyday snacking routine.
