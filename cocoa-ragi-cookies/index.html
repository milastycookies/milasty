Cocoa Ragi Cookies

Ingredients

Ragi flour, jaggery, whole wheat flour (atta), desi cow ghee, rice bran oil, milk, cocoa powder, pumpkin seeds, baking powder, baking soda, salt, vanilla essence.

Allergen Information

Contains whole wheat (atta) (gluten), milk, and pumpkin seeds. Manufactured in a home kitchen where other nuts and seeds may be handled.

Why you’ll love it

  • Made with ragi (finger millet), a traditional grain valued for its natural fibre content and wholesome nourishment, making it a thoughtful choice for everyday family snacking.
  • Sweetened with jaggery instead of refined sugar, offering a gentler sweetness that feels balanced and familiar.
  • Prepared using desi cow ghee along with rice bran oil, avoiding palm oil and heavily refined fats.
  • Contains whole wheat flour (atta), not refined wheat (maida), keeping the grain closer to its natural form.
  • Baked in small batches with no preservatives, no artificial colours, and no flavour enhancers — just simple ingredients you can recognise and trust, every day.
