Coconut Jowar Cookies

Homemade • Small batch baked • Made with desi ghee

Ingredients

Jowar flour (sorghum), jaggery, whole wheat flour (atta), desiccated coconut, desi cow ghee, rice bran oil, milk powder, sesame seeds, baking powder, baking soda, salt.

Allergen Information

Contains whole wheat (atta) (gluten), milk, coconut, and sesame seeds. Manufactured in a home kitchen where other nuts and seeds may be handled.

Why you’ll love it

  • Made with jowar (sorghum), a traditional millet known for its naturally high fibre content and suitability for everyday diets.
  • Enriched with desiccated coconut, adding gentle sweetness, texture, and a comforting homemade flavour.
  • Sweetened with jaggery instead of refined sugar, keeping the taste balanced and familiar.
  • Prepared using desi cow ghee along with rice bran oil, avoiding palm oil and heavily refined fats.
  • Contains whole wheat flour (atta), not refined wheat (maida), keeping the grain closer to its natural form.
  • Baked in small batches with no preservatives, no artificial colours, and no flavour enhancers — just simple ingredients you can recognise and trust, every day.

Thoughtfully baked for gentle, everyday nourishment.

Who is this for?

  • Families and parents looking for a wholesome snack option made with traditional grains and familiar ingredients.
  • New and young mothers who prefer gentle, homemade-style foods prepared in small batches with simple ingredients.
  • Fitness-conscious and active individuals who choose whole grains over refined ingredients for everyday snacking.
  • Anyone who enjoys mindful eating and values transparency in how their food is made.

Enjoy as part of your everyday snacking routine.
