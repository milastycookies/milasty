Ingredient Transparency

  • ✔ Millets: Ragi, Jowar, Bajra
  • ✔ Sweetened with Jaggery (no refined sugar)
  • ✔ Desi ghee + small amount of rice bran oil
  • ✔ Small quantity of whole wheat (no maida)

⚠️ These cookies are not gluten-free as they contain a small amount of whole wheat.

Why Choose Milasty Cookies?

  • Better grains than refined flour cookies
  • No refined sugar or artificial ingredients

  • Balanced sweetness—not overly sugary
  • Ideal for daily snacking & mindful gifting
