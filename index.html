Why Milasty Exists

Milasty started in a home kitchen with a simple frustration—most “healthy” cookies didn’t taste good, and most tasty cookies didn’t feel good to eat.

By experimenting with millets, jaggery, and desi ghee, and baking in small batches, these cookies were refined through honest feedback from colleagues and neighbours.

Every Milasty cookie is still baked with the same mindset—no shortcuts, no factory-style production, just thoughtful ingredients and care in every batch.
