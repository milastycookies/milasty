Our Story

Hi, I’m Anwesha.

Milasty didn’t begin as a business plan. It began in my kitchen, during a phase when my health asked me to slow down and choose better.

Every recipe here carries that intention — to make food that cares, without asking you to compromise on taste.

I’m an engineer by education — I completed my BTech in 2020 and, like many young professionals, stepped into the corporate world soon after college.

During COVID, working from home slowed life down. It gave me space to cook, reflect, and listen to my body. But as offices reopened, long working hours away from home returned, and food once again became rushed and convenient.

Like many working professionals, I felt stuck between two choices — food that was healthy but boring, or food that tasted good but quietly harmed health. Over time, my health paid the price. I was diagnosed with thyroid issues, PCOD, and lived under constant mental stress.

Stepping away from my job wasn’t easy, but it gave me clarity. I chose to return to our roots and to food our bodies understand.

That’s when I rediscovered traditional millets — simple, wholesome, naturally high in fibre, gluten-free, and truly filling. As I started cooking for myself, food began to feel nourishing again.

Around the same time, I began baking healthy cookies for my husband, who works in the corporate world and has been my strongest support system. The intention was simple — to help him manage workday cravings without relying on junk food.

Soon, his colleagues started tasting the cookies. They liked them. Then they asked for more. What started in my kitchen, for one person, slowly reached many.

That’s how Milasty was born.

Today, our cookies are made without maida, palm oil, or processed sugar — using millets, jaggery, and desi ghee.

Because good food shouldn’t feel like a compromise.
It should feel like home.
