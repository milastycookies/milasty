Privacy Policy

Your trust matters. Here’s exactly how MILASTY handles your data 🤎

Last Updated: January 2026

🌿 Our Promise

MILASTY respects your privacy. We collect only the minimum information required to deliver your cookies safely and provide customer support.

✅ We do not sell or misuse your personal data — ever.

📌 What Information We Collect

When you place an order through WhatsApp or contact us, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your delivery address
  • Order details (products, quantity)
  • Payment confirmation (only transaction reference)

🍪 Why We Collect This Data

We collect this information only for genuine business purposes such as:

  • Confirming your order
  • Shipping cookies to your address
  • Providing order updates
  • Customer support and complaint resolution

🚚 Sharing Information with Courier Partners

To deliver your order, we may share limited information such as your name, phone number, and address with trusted courier services.

📦 Courier partners receive only what is necessary for delivery.

🔒 How We Protect Your Information

MILASTY takes customer privacy seriously. We ensure that:

  • Your information is accessed only by authorized personnel
  • Order details are used only for delivery and support
  • We never store sensitive card or banking details

📢 Marketing Communication

We may occasionally send updates about new MILASTY products or offers. You can always opt out by simply messaging us “STOP”.

✅ No spam. Only meaningful updates, and only if you’re comfortable.

👤 Your Rights

You have full control over your data. You may request:

  • To see what data we have about you
  • Correction of incorrect details
  • Deletion of your information (after order completion)

🌐 Website Cookies & Tracking

MILASTY website is brochure-style and does not use invasive tracking cookies. We do not collect unnecessary browsing data.

⚖️ Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Questions about your privacy or data? Message us anytime ❤️
