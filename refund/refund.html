Refund & Replacement Policy

Simple, fair, and transparent — because MILASTY values trust 🍪

Last Updated: January 2026

🍪 Food Products Cannot Be Returned

MILASTY sells edible food products. For hygiene and safety reasons, we do not accept returns once an order has been delivered.

✅ No return policy helps ensure food safety for all customers.

📦 When Replacement or Refund is Possible

We will happily provide a replacement or refund only in genuine cases such as:

  • Product received in damaged condition
  • Wrong product delivered
  • Package arrived tampered or opened

⏱ Reporting Time Limit

If there is an issue with your order, please inform us within 24 hours of delivery.

📸 Photo or video proof is required for faster resolution.

🚚 Courier Damages & Delays

We pack all cookies carefully. However, courier handling is outside our control. If damage occurs during transit, we will assist you fairly based on proof shared.

🎥 Unboxing Video Requirement (Highly Recommended)

To ensure a smooth and fair resolution in case of damage, we strongly recommend customers record a short video while opening the package for the first time.

✅ An unboxing video helps us verify courier-related damage quickly and process replacements or refunds faster.

Claims for damaged or missing items may not be accepted without proper photo/video proof.

💳 Refund Timeline

Approved refunds will be processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method (UPI/Bank Transfer).

❌ Order Cancellation Policy

Since MILASTY cookies are prepared and packed soon after confirmation, cancellations are accepted only within a limited time window.

  • Within 3 hours of payment confirmation → ✅ Full refund
  • Between 3 to 6 hours → ✅ 50% refund (packing may have started)
  • After 6 hours → ❌ Cancellation not possible
The cancellation window is calculated from the time the payment is successfully completed.

Refunds (if approved) are processed within 5–7 business days via original payment method.
