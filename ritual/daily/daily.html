Why Choose Millet Snacks for Everyday Tea Time?

Millets like ragi, jowar and bajra have been part of traditional Indian diets for centuries. Today they are rediscovered as wholesome grains for everyday snacking. Millet snacks are increasingly preferred as alternatives to maida based biscuits and refined flour cookies. MILASTY millet bakes are crafted to bring these ancient grains into modern tea rituals. Benefits of Millet-Based Snacks

  • Rich in natural fibre
  • Slow energy release compared to refined flour snacks
  • Traditionally grown grains suited for Indian climates
  • Pairs beautifully with tea and coffee

Which Millet Cookie Should You Try First?

If you enjoy warm spiced flavours, Cardamom Bajra cookies are a comforting choice. For lighter texture, Coconut Jowar offers a delicate bite. Chocolate lovers often prefer Cocoa Ragi for its deeper cocoa richness.
