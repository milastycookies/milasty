Why Make Millets Part of Your Daily Ritual?

Millet-based bakes are a wholesome alternative to refined flour snacks, offering deeper flavour, better texture, and a more nourishing experience.

Traditional Indian millets such as ragi, jowar, and bajra have nourished generations. As whole grains, they retain their natural structure, depth, and character. When slow baked with jaggery and pure ghee, they create a grounding bite that feels less like a casual snack and more like a mindful daily ritual.

Compared to conventional snacks made with maida and refined sugar, millet-based bakes offer greater substance, natural satiety, and a more sustaining experience — making them ideal for a daily tea ritual, a focused work pause, or a light meal moment.

Ritual Questions

Are MILASTY cookies made with maida?

No. MILASTY millet bakes are crafted using ragi, jowar, and bajra, along with a small quantity of whole wheat atta. We do not use refined flour (maida).

Do you use refined sugar in your baked snacks?

No. Our millet cookies are sweetened with natural jaggery, not refined sugar. This creates a deeper flavour and a more balanced sweetness.

Are MILASTY cookies baked in desi ghee?

Yes. We bake with pure desi cow ghee, complemented with a small amount of rice bran oil for balance.

Do MILASTY cookies contain preservatives or palm oil?

No. We do not use palm oil, artificial preservatives, added colours, or flavour enhancers. Our bakes are prepared in small batches for freshness.

Which millets are used in MILASTY products?

We use heritage Indian millets such as ragi, jowar, and bajra. These whole grains bring natural texture, depth, and substance to every ritual bite.

Are millet bakes suitable for daily consumption?

Yes. MILASTY millet bakes are designed as a mindful daily tea ritual — offering natural satiety and a more sustaining alternative to refined flour snacks.
