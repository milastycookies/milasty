Shipping Policy

Freshly packed MILASTY cookies delivered across India 🚚🍪

Last Updated: February 2026

📦 Shipping Coverage

MILASTY ships cookies across India through trusted third-party courier partners. We carefully pack every order to ensure safe delivery.

✅ Pan-India delivery available (subject to courier serviceability).

⏱ Dispatch Timeline

Orders are usually prepared and dispatched within:

  • 1–2 business days after successful payment
  • Dispatch may take longer during high-demand periods or holidays

🚚 Estimated Delivery Time

Delivery timelines depend on your location and courier availability. Estimated delivery is:

  • 3–7 business days for most Indian cities
  • Remote locations may take slightly longer
Courier delays due to weather, operations, or regional issues are beyond MILASTY’s control.

📍 Address & Contact Details

Customers are responsible for providing the correct shipping address and phone number. Incorrect details may lead to delivery failure or additional courier charges.

📲 Tracking Information

If tracking is available from the courier partner, we will share tracking details via WhatsApp once the order is shipped.

✅ WhatsApp is our primary communication channel for shipping updates.

📦 Packaging & Safety

MILASTY cookies are packed with care to maintain freshness and reduce breakage. However, minor breakage during transit may occur due to courier handling.

❗ Damaged Packages

If your order arrives damaged or tampered, please contact us within 24 hours with photo/video proof.

🎥 We highly recommend recording an unboxing video while opening the package, as it helps us resolve issues quickly.
